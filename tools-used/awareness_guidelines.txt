Phishing Awareness Guidelines

Purpose:
These guidelines help employees identify phishing attempts and avoid credential theft, malware infections, and social engineering attacks.

How Phishing Attacks Work

Phishing attackers attempt to trick users into:
- clicking malicious links
- downloading infected attachments
- sharing passwords or OTPs
- revealing sensitive information

Attackers commonly use fake emails that imitate trusted organizations.

Common Phishing Indicators

1. Suspicious sender domains
2. Urgent or threatening language
3. Generic greetings
4. Suspicious links
5. Unexpected attachments
6. Requests for passwords or OTPs
7. Poor grammar or spelling mistakes

Employee Safety Tips

- Verify sender email addresses carefully
- Hover over links before clicking
- Never share passwords through email
- Avoid downloading unexpected attachments
- Confirm urgent requests through official channels
- Report suspicious emails to the IT/security team

Do's
- Verify domains before clicking links
- Use strong passwords and MFA
- Report suspicious emails immediately
- Contact official support teams directly

Don'ts
- Do not click unknown links
- Do not share OTPs through email
- Do not trust urgent scare tactics
- Do not open suspicious attachments

Conclusion

User awareness is one of the strongest defenses against phishing attacks. Regular security awareness training can significantly reduce the success rate of phishing campaigns.
